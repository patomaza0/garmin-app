Pulse

Connect to your data

This app reads your private garmin-data repository with a GitHub access token. You only do this once per device; the token stays on this device.

  1. Open github.com/settings/personal-access-tokens/new (Fine-grained token).
  2. Name it Pulse app, expiration up to 1 year, Only select repositories → garmin-data.
  3. Repository permissions: Contents: Read-only and Actions: Read and write. Nothing else.
  4. Generate, copy, paste below.

Zone uses each lap's average heart rate. On short reps the heart lags behind the effort, so max HR shows how hard a rep really got. Cad. is cadence in steps per minute.

Recovery combines resting heart rate against your 30-day normal (45%), sleep against the target (30%) and Body Battery (25%). Strain is the day's Garmin training load on a 0–21 scale. Both are this app's own scores in the style of WHOOP; the Forerunner 945 doesn't record overnight HRV.
